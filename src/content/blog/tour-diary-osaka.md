Last weekend we played three shows in Osaka and it was absolutely wonderful. Here's a quick recap of the trip.

## Day 1 — Shinsaibashi JANUS

The first show was packed. The crowd sang along to every word of "Glider" and we had to play an extra encore. Afterwards we found a tiny okonomiyaki place and stayed until midnight.

## Day 2 — Umeda Shangri-La

A more relaxed set with some acoustic arrangements. Aiko tried out the new song "Paper Plane" live for the first time and the response was incredible.

## Day 3 — Namba Bears

The final night was electric. Pure energy from start to finish. We closed with "Red Bicycle" and the whole room was jumping.

Thank you Osaka — we'll be back soon!
