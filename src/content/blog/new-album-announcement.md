We're thrilled to share that our new album is nearly finished! After months of recording at our favourite studio in Koenji, the tracks are being mixed and we couldn't be more excited about how they sound.

The album features 11 new songs — a mix of upbeat indie pop, a couple of dreamy slower numbers, and one surprise collaboration we can't reveal just yet.

## What to Expect

The sound leans into the jangly guitar tones we love, with some new textures from organ and mellotron. Think early morning sunshine filtered through a bedroom window.

Release date and pre-order details coming soon. Stay tuned!
